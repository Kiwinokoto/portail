# ADA Mathématiques — numératie fondamentale V1

## Audience and boundary

This is not the CAP PSR maths diagnostic simplified cosmetically. It is a separate foundation pathway for adults who may have fragile schooling, limited literacy or uncertain number knowledge.

The first pass deliberately asks small, concrete questions. It does not produce a global score.

## Learner sequence

```text
prepared identity
→ concrete quantity
→ spoken number to written digit
→ compare two quantities
→ concrete addition
→ written money amount
→ finish
```

Each completed probe is stored as its own activity item. The overall completion item is `numeracy-v1`.

## Probe meanings

### Concrete quantity — `quantity-counting`

The learner hears “touche le groupe avec trois assiettes” and chooses among visual groups.

**Signal:** selecting a requested small concrete quantity.

**Do not infer:** general counting range, numeral reading or arithmetic.

### Spoken number → digit — `spoken-number`

The learner hears “sept” and selects the written digit 7.

**Signal:** recognition of one spoken number in written numeral form.

**Do not infer:** place value, number line understanding or calculation.

### Compare quantities — `compare-quantities`

The learner sees two groups of glasses and is asked which has more.

**Signal:** comparison of two small visible quantities.

Formal vocabulary such as supérieur/inférieur is intentionally absent at this stage.

### Concrete addition — `concrete-addition`

Two plates plus one plate are shown visually; the learner chooses how many there are altogether.

**Signal:** solving one simple additive situation with concrete visual support.

**Do not infer:** written addition technique or mastery of operations.

### Money amount — `money-amount`

The learner hears “cinq euros” and selects `5 €` among a few written amounts.

**Signal:** recognition of one spoken monetary amount in written form.

**Do not infer:** making change, decimal money calculation or budgeting.

## Shared teacher infrastructure

ADA Maths reuses the same mechanisms as ADA French:

- teacher-created class sessions;
- preloaded roster and signed learner QR/link;
- safe roster correction;
- corrections state;
- 4-second live follow-up;
- teacher preview with no event writes;
- Reports V1 with per-learner and per-item detail;
- descriptive same-session group comparison.

The live view expects six completion items for this V1: the five probes plus the overall `numeracy-v1` completion.

## Teacher preview

Authenticated teachers use **ADA → Mathématiques → Parcours**. Preview runs with a fake learner in browser memory and no signed join token, so no learner event is written.

## Classroom validation still required

Before expanding this pathway:

1. verify the visual groups and emoji rendering on the actual learner devices;
2. listen to the French TTS for numbers and short instructions;
3. observe whether learners understand “en tout” and “le plus” without teacher reformulation;
4. check whether written € amounts are visually clear on small screens;
5. use observed difficulties to decide the next practice modules: counting, numeral formation, addition/subtraction, money, time and measurement.

The next modules should provide repeated practice, not turn this V1 positioning into a high-stakes test.
